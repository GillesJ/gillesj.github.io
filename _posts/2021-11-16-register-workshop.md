 <li> If your car is not allowed, we recommend coming by train or <a href="https://visit.gent.be/en/good-know/practical-information/how-get-ghent/car/pr-car-parks">parking in the P+R car parks</a> (spec. Flanders Expo). Next take `Tram 1` to the city center.</li>
