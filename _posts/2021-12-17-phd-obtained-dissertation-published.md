Yesterday, I successfully defended his PhD on Extracting Fine-Grained Event and Sentiment from Economic News.
I was supervised by prof. Véronique Hoste, prof. Els Lefever and prof. Diane Breesch.
The actual defence was preceded by a [workshop on "NLP for Economics"](https://jacobsgill.es/workshop) with around 40 people attending.

The past years I have worked on machine learning-based approaches for obtaining structured factual data alongside subjective information from business news for use in financial applications.
To this purpose I created an extensive dataset for fine-grained event extraction and sentiment analysis in economic news, named SENTiVENT.
This novel resource was validated with machine learning experiments in which state-of-the-art deep learning models were applied in coarse- and fine-grained settings.
