<summary markdown='span'>⯈ <ins>How to reach Het Pand.</ins></summary>

**By public transport**
- Take train to Gent-Sint-Pieter main station.
- Take `Tram 1 Gent Flanders Expo - Wondelgem Industrieweg` -> Stop Gent Korenmarkt (11min) -> Walk to Het Pand (450m)
- Or take `Tram 2 towards Zwijnaarde - Melle Leeuw` -> Stop Gent Oude Houtlei (11min) -> Walk to Het Pand (400m)

**By car**: Het Pand is located centrally in Ghent's Low Emission Zone (LEZ). See [here for more info on parking](https://stad.gent/nl/mobiliteit-openbare-werken/parkeren/parkings-gent).
 - [If your car is allowed in the LEZ](https://stad.gent/en/mobility-ghent/low-emission-zone-ghent) the Parking P1 Vrijdagsmarkt is a good option. The smaller parking lots nearer to Het Pand (P6 Center, P7 St-Michiels, P8 Ramen) are often full.
 
- If your car is not allowed, we recommend coming by train or [parking in the P+R car parks (spec. Flanders Expo)](https://visit.gent.be/en/good-know/practical-information/how-get-ghent/car/pr-car-parks). Next take `Tram 1` to the city center.
